# Positioning Strategy: HuellaCam (Peru)

## Target Segment
Urban pet parents in Peru—starting with busy professionals and apartment dwellers in Lima—who leave dogs or cats at home during work and commute hours.

## Core Positioning Statement
For urban pet parents in Peru—starting with busy professionals and apartment dwellers in Lima—who worry about leaving pets alone during long commutes and workdays, **HuellaCam** is a smart pet camera that combines two-way check-ins, real-time bark/activity alerts, and optional no-forced-lock-in cloud clips at an accessible price point, unlike pricey imported pet cams with mandatory subscriptions or rigid home-security cameras that miss pet moments.
