# Market Research: Smart Pet Camera in Peru

## Overview
Urban Peruvian pet households—led by Lima—increasingly treat companion animals as family members while balancing long commutes, work hours, and budget consciousness.

## Key Findings
- **Demand Drivers:** Remote check-ins during workdays, two-way audio reassurance, bark/motion alerts, and treat/play features appeal to busy apartment and urban home owners.
- **Price & Subscription Sensitivity:** Compared to North American and European markets, Peruvian buyers show strong price sensitivity and resistance to mandatory high monthly cloud fees.
- **Competitive Landscape:** Low-cost generic home security cameras compete on price, while premium global pet cameras compete on specialized pet features and app polish.
- **Adoption Friction:** Upfront cost, recurring subscriptions, Wi-Fi/power reliability, and indoor camera privacy concerns can stall purchase decisions.
